## How to use this file

Run the Nafisa asset library first, once, before any page sessions. This builds
the image bank every page draws from for practitioner shots.

Then run each page session individually. One page per Claude Code session.

For every generation in this file: confirm the free toggle is ON in Higgsfield
before generating. Free models: Flux.2 Pro, GPT Image, Seedream 4.5, Kling O1
Image, Nano Banana, Seedream 5.0 Lite.

Before-and-after images already exist in assets/web/ as real client photography.
Do not regenerate these. They are never a Higgsfield placement.

## Nafisa asset library

```
Run the Higgsfield asset generation skill for the PureMed Nafisa asset library.
This is not a page session. It builds the central image bank for the site.

Soul character name: Nafisa
Discovery: ~/workspace/other-projects/puremed/discovery/constraints.md
Asset output: ~/workspace/other-projects/puremed/assets/web/

This session generates at least 2 shots per free model across the scenarios
below. Minimum 12 images total. Run each scenario through all 6 free models
unless the model is clearly unsuitable for the scene type.

Free models (confirm free toggle ON for each):
Flux.2 Pro, GPT Image, Seedream 4.5, Kling O1 Image, Nano Banana, Seedream 5.0 Lite

Environment for all shots:
Modern aesthetic medical clinic. Bright white walls, clean minimal decor, large
window providing natural daylight from left of frame. Navy blue accent details
in decor — cushions, trim, signage. Medical treatment chair or consultation desk
present. PureMed logo visible as framed wall signage in background, partially
in frame, never centred or dominant. Bright and evenly lit throughout. No
dramatic directional lighting, no shadows, no dark tones anywhere.

Lighting for all shots:
Soft natural daylight from left, supplemented by clean overhead clinical light.
Bright, airy, and evenly distributed. The feeling of a well-lit private clinic
on a clear day. Not cinematic. Not moody. Not studio-lit with harsh highlights.

Scenarios — generate at least 2 variants per free model across these:

SCENARIO A — Consultation
Nafisa seated at a white consultation desk, facing a female patient aged 40-50
seated across from her. Nafisa in white clinical uniform. Patient in casual
smart clothing. Both looking at a tablet or consultation notes on the desk.
Nafisa's expression: engaged, warm, professional. Shot framed from slightly
to the side, both women visible. PureMed logo signage partially visible on
wall behind.

SCENARIO B — Pre-treatment assessment
Nafisa standing beside a patient who is reclined in a white treatment chair.
Nafisa examining the patient's face with gloved hands, gentle clinical focus.
Patient's eyes relaxed or lightly closed, expression calm and trusting. Shot
from side-on, treatment room environment visible. Clean treatment trolley with
products in background.

SCENARIO C — Treatment in progress
Nafisa performing an injectable treatment. Clinical precision — small syringe
held correctly, focused expression, gloved. Patient reclined in treatment chair,
expression neutral and comfortable. Bright treatment room. Shot framed tight
on the clinical interaction, not the needle itself. PureMed logo on wall or
visible on product packaging on trolley in background.

SCENARIO D — Results review
Nafisa and patient both looking at a hand mirror or a screen showing results.
Patient's expression: pleased, natural, not dramatic. Nafisa's expression:
professional pride, warm. Both standing. Bright consultation room. Shot from
slightly behind and to the side, faces and reactions visible.

SCENARIO E — Clinical portrait, Nafisa only
Nafisa standing in the clinic, facing slightly off-camera. White clinical
uniform. Arms relaxed. Expression: confident and approachable, a natural
slight smile. Clean white wall behind her, treatment room environment implied.
PureMed logo signage visible in background. This is the hero portrait for
the site — generate across all 6 free models for maximum variants.

SCENARIO F — Skin close-up (no character, environmental)
Extreme close-up of healthy, luminous female skin. Cheek or jawline area.
Soft natural sidelight, skin texture visible but beautiful — not a problem
to be solved. No makeup product, no treatment device, just skin. Shot as if
on a high-end skincare editorial. Neutral warm background, slightly out of
focus.

File naming for all outputs:
nafisa-library-[scenario-letter]-[model-shortname]-v[n].webp
Example: nafisa-library-a-flux2pro-v1.webp
```
