The Treatment

What Are Anti-Wrinkle
Injections?

Anti-wrinkle injections use a highly purified protein to temporarily relax specific facial muscles — reducing the appearance of dynamic lines caused by repeated expressions such as frowning, squinting, or smiling.

When delivered by an experienced practitioner like Nafisa, the result is a smoother, more rested appearance that still moves naturally. The goal is never a frozen face — it is a refreshed one.

Treatment takes around 20 minutes. Results begin to show within 3–5 days and are fully visible at 2 weeks.

Anti-wrinkle injections are suitable for adults who want to reduce or prevent the appearance of expression lines.

Those with forehead lines, frown lines (11 lines), or crow's feet

Clients wanting a subtle brow lift or more open, rested eyes

Anyone looking to slim the jawline or reduce teeth-grinding tension

People interested in a preventative approach to ageing well

Clients wanting a quick, low-downtime treatment with natural results

The Process

How It Works

A precise, personalised procedure delivered at our Winslow clinic.

1

Consultation & Assessment

Nafisa assesses your facial movement, muscle strength and skin quality to determine the right dose and placement for your goals.

2

Preparation

The treatment area is cleansed. A topical anaesthetic can be applied if preferred, though most clients find the injections very comfortable without it.

3

Injection

Using ultra-fine needles, Nafisa places precise doses into the targeted muscles. The treatment typically takes 15–20 minutes.

4

Review at 2 Weeks

A complimentary review appointment is offered at 2 weeks to assess the result and make any minor adjustments if needed.

5

Maintenance

Results last 3–4 months. Regular appointments maintain consistent results and can reduce the amount needed over time.
