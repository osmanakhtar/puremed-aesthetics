The Treatment

What Is
Laser Lift?

Laser Lift is our consumer-friendly name for Endolift — a minimally invasive laser treatment that delivers targeted energy beneath the skin to tighten, lift, and stimulate long-term collagen production.

A very fine fibre-optic probe — thinner than a hair — is introduced just below the surface of the skin. The laser energy contracts the tissue immediately and triggers a natural healing response that builds new collagen over the following months.

The result is a naturally tighter, lifted appearance that looks like you on a very good day — not someone who has had work done. Most clients see an initial result within a few weeks, with peak results at 3–6 months.

Ideal Candidates

Who Is It
Right For?

Laser Lift is most effective for clients who are beginning to notice skin laxity and want to address it without surgery.

Women aged 35–65 noticing early to moderate skin laxity in the face and neck

Clients wanting to prevent or reverse sagging around the jawline and jowls

Those looking for an alternative to surgical facelift with less risk and recovery

Clients who want gradual, natural results that don't signal "I've had work done"

Anyone with good overall skin health and realistic expectations about outcomes

Not suitable for everyone

Laser Lift may not be appropriate if you are pregnant, have certain skin conditions, active infections in the treatment area, or are taking specific medications. A full medical consultation with Nafisa will determine your suitability before any treatment proceeds.

The Process

How Laser Lift
Works

A precise, medical-grade procedure carried out in a single session at our Winslow clinic.

1

Consultation & Assessment

Nafisa assesses your skin laxity, facial anatomy, and goals. She'll confirm your suitability and design your treatment plan — no treatment proceeds without this step.

2

Preparation & Anaesthetic

Topical anaesthetic cream is applied 30–45 minutes before the procedure to ensure comfort. The treatment area is cleansed and marked.

3

Laser Delivery

A fine fibre-optic probe is introduced just beneath the skin. Controlled laser energy targets the tissue, triggering immediate contraction and stimulating collagen production.

4

Immediate Aftercare

The area is cooled and soothed. Nafisa reviews the result with you and provides detailed aftercare instructions for the days following your treatment.

5

Review & Follow Up

A follow-up appointment is scheduled to assess your results and ensure you're healing well. We're available throughout your recovery.

What to Expect

Results & Recovery

Most clients find Laser Lift straightforward with minimal disruption to daily life.

Day 1–3
Initial Healing

Mild swelling and tenderness in the treated area. Some redness is normal. Avoid strenuous activity and sun exposure.

Week 1–2
Settled & Visible

Swelling subsides. Initial tightening becomes visible. Most clients feel comfortable resuming normal activities by day 5–7.

Month 1–3
Collagen Building

Natural collagen production increases. Skin becomes progressively firmer, smoother, and more defined throughout this period.

Month 3–6+
Peak Results

Full collagen remodelling complete. Peak results visible — natural lift and tightening that can last 12–18 months or longer.
