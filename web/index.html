All treatments are carried out by Nafisa Mughal, medically trained with a dental background and advanced anatomy knowledge. We use only premium, clinically proven products and conduct a thorough consultation before any treatment.
Natural results are the only results we deliver. Nafisa's philosophy is to enhance what you have: refresh, restore, and tighten, without changing who you are. Her 90 five-star reviews reflect this consistently.
Laser Lift (Endolift) is our flagship skin tightening treatment. A fine fibre-optic laser placed just beneath the skin stimulates collagen and tightens from within. Results are natural and continue improving over 3–6 months as new collagen forms.
It depends on the treatment and your goals. Many clients see results from a single Laser Lift or Liquid Facelift session. Collagen-stimulating treatments like RF Microneedling typically work best as a course of 3. We build a plan at consultation.
Most treatments have minimal to no downtime. Some mild redness or swelling for 24–48 hours after injectable or RF treatments is normal. The Laser Lift may have a few days of mild swelling. We discuss this fully in your consultation.
