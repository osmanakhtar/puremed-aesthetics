The Treatment

What Is a
Liquid Facelift?

A liquid facelift uses hyaluronic acid dermal fillers — a substance naturally found in the skin — to restore lost volume, smooth lines, and redefine facial contours.

Unlike a surgical facelift, there are no incisions. Nafisa carefully maps your facial anatomy and injects fillers into key areas to achieve a lifted, balanced result that looks entirely natural.

Results are visible immediately and continue to settle over the following two weeks. Most clients describe looking refreshed and rested rather than "done".

The Process

How It Works

A precise, personalised procedure delivered at our Winslow clinic.

1

Consultation & Assessment

Nafisa assesses your facial anatomy, skin quality and goals. She'll discuss which areas would benefit most and explain exactly what to expect.

2

Mapping & Preparation

The treatment areas are marked and a topical anaesthetic cream applied for comfort. Most fillers also contain lidocaine for additional pain relief.

3

Filler Placement

Using fine needles or cannulas, Nafisa precisely injects filler into the agreed areas. She reviews and adjusts throughout for a balanced, natural result.

4

Immediate Review

You'll see the results straight away. Nafisa reviews the outcome with you and provides detailed aftercare guidance.

5

Follow-Up

A follow-up is offered at 2 weeks to review the settled result and make any minor adjustments if needed.
