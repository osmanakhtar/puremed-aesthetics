What is Sculptra?

Not a filler. A collagen-stimulating rejuvenation.

Sculptra (poly-L-lactic acid) works with your body — gently signalling your skin to produce its own new collagen over several months. Rather than "plumping" an area like a traditional filler, it rebuilds the deep foundations of your face.

The result: firmer, fresher, more sculpted skin — because the lift is genuinely your own.

Sculptra vs. Traditional Filler

Why Sculptra is different

Both are injectable. That's where the similarity ends. Understanding the difference is the single most important thing if you want natural-looking results.

Traditional Hyaluronic Filler

  • Adds volume by physically filling space
  • Instant — but can look obvious or heavy
  • Risk of pillow-face or "overfilled" look
  • Doesn't improve skin quality or firmness
  • Typically lasts 6–12 months

Sculptra · Bio-Stimulator

  • Stimulates your own collagen production
  • Gradual, subtle results — no sudden change
  • Improves skin quality, firmness & glow
  • Restores facial structure & lift naturally
  • Results can last up to 2 years

FAQs

What women always ask us

If your question isn't answered here, message Nafisa directly on WhatsApp — she'll always reply personally.

Will Sculptra make me look "done" or overfilled? +
No. This is exactly why so many of our patients choose Sculptra. Because it stimulates your own collagen rather than physically adding volume in one area, the change is gradual and structural. People will notice you look refreshed, but they won't be able to tell why.
How is Sculptra different from dermal filler? +
Traditional hyaluronic acid filler physically occupies space under the skin to "plump" an area. Sculptra is a bio-stimulator — it triggers your own collagen production, rebuilding the deep foundations of the face. The lift you see is genuinely your own tissue.
How long until I see results? +
Sculptra is deliberately gradual. Most patients notice subtle improvements within 4–6 weeks, with the full result developing over 3–6 months as your collagen rebuilds. The pay-off is that the rejuvenation looks beautifully natural — no sudden change for anyone to comment on.
How long do the results last? +
Sculptra results can last up to 2 years, which is significantly longer than most traditional fillers. Many patients top up annually to maintain their results.
How many sessions will I need? +
Most patients require 2–3 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart for optimal results. Your personalised plan will be confirmed during your consultation, based on your facial anatomy, goals and the degree of volume loss.
Is there any downtime? +
Minimal. You may notice small areas of redness, mild swelling or pinpoint bruising for 24–48 hours. Most patients return to normal activities immediately. Full aftercare guidance is provided.
Is Sculptra safe? +
Sculptra has been used clinically for over two decades and is fully regulated. Safety depends overwhelmingly on the practitioner's anatomical knowledge and technique — which is why Nafisa's medical and dental background, combined with her advanced training, is so reassuring.
How much does Sculptra cost? +
Pricing is bespoke because every face and every plan is different. Your consultation is a stand-alone £25 fee and includes a full facial assessment, an honest recommendation and a transparent, written treatment plan with costs — so you can decide in your own time, with no pressure.
Am I a suitable candidate? +
Sculptra is ideal for women noticing loss of volume, mild sagging, jowling or skin laxity who want a natural, structural rejuvenation. The best way to find out if it's right for you is a private consultation with Nafisa — and if Sculptra isn't the answer for you, she'll tell you honestly.
