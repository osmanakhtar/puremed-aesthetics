What is real here
The treatment list, prices and appointment durations are PureMed's live 2026 price list. The booking and cancellation policy is the clinic's own published wording, used verbatim.
What is placeholder
Consent and medical history wording is marked Placeholder throughout. Real clinical forms have not been supplied yet and inventing plausible clinical wording would make this look more finished than it is. Deposit percentages are a studio assumption, not clinic policy.
What is not built
No server, no database, no calendar connection, no payment provider. Availability is computed live in the browser from a fixed working pattern and a fixed set of busy blocks, standing in for a real calendar sync.

Every requirement the client is shown appears here first, with the rule that produced it. Nothing is asked for that cannot be explained by a numbered row. This is the compliance artefact, not a debug view.

Regulatory note
Nothing in this prototype has had legal or compliance review. It demonstrates a design approach, it is not a compliance assurance. Health data handling, lawful basis and retention are specified in the architecture plan and require the practice's own review before any live use.
Determinism
No randomness, no inference, no language model in the decision path. Availability is a pure function of the working pattern, imported busy time, buffers and notice period. Requirements are a pure function of the service selected and the practice's settings.
