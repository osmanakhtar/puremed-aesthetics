## A few things worth knowing

**Your old emails are here.** Everything from the previous inbox was migrated across, including Sent, Drafts, and older messages. If something looks missing, check All Mail in the left sidebar — Gmail archives rather than deletes.

**Spam lives in a different place.** Gmail calls it Spam, not Junk. Same thing. Check it occasionally for anything that might have been caught by mistake.

**Search is excellent.** Gmail's search is fast and thorough. If you can't find something, search for it before assuming it's missing.

**Two-step verification is on.** Your account is protected by 2-step verification, which sends a code to your registered phone when you sign in from a new device. This is a security feature, not a problem.

## Managing your account — the admin console

As the account owner, you have access to the Google Workspace admin console at [admin.google.com](https://admin.google.com). Sign in with `care@puremed.uk`.

This is where you manage the account itself, as distinct from your email inbox. The main things you'll use it for:

**Users:** Your account currently has one user, `care@puremed.uk`. You can view, edit, or reset the password for this account from the Users section.

**Billing:** Your subscription details, billing date, and payment method are under Billing in the admin console. Google Workspace Business Starter is billed monthly per user.

**Security:** Two-step verification settings, account recovery options, and password policies live under Security.

You don't need to use the admin console day-to-day. It's there when something needs changing at the account level rather than the inbox level.

## Adding a new user

Your account is on Google Workspace Business Starter, which is priced per user. Currently you have one user.

To add a new user:

1. Go to [admin.google.com](https://admin.google.com)
2. Click Users in the left menu
3. Click Add new user
4. Enter the name and the email address you want to create (for example, `hello@puremed.uk`)
5. Set a temporary password — the new user will be prompted to change it on first sign-in

**Cost implication:** Each additional user adds one licence to your monthly bill. Business Starter is currently £7 per user per month (excluding VAT) on a flexible monthly plan. Adding one user takes your monthly cost from £7 to £14, plus VAT.

There is no minimum user requirement. You can add and remove users at any time, and your bill adjusts the following month.

## If something isn't right

**Can't sign in:** Go to [accounts.google.com](https://accounts.google.com) and use the Forgot password option. You'll need access to your recovery email or phone number.

**Missing emails:** Check All Mail first. If something still looks missing, contact Main Stage Studio before making any changes.

**Technical issues with the account:** Google Workspace support is available at [support.google.com/a](https://support.google.com/a). Your account is on the Business Starter plan.

**Anything else:** Get in touch with Main Stage Studio and we'll pick it up from there.
